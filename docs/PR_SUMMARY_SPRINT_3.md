# Pull Request Summary: Sprint 3

Sprint 3 adds the source audit and proposed dataset changes for:

- Tower Semiconductor's current Israeli fabrication operations
- Israel's AI data-center planning framework
- Electricity-grid connection constraints
- Government Decision 4255 corroboration
- AI21 private-company control uncertainty

## Main conclusions

1. Tower Fab 2 is an operational Israeli specialty foundry capability; Fab 1 production ceased in 2025.
2. Tower must not be represented as leading-edge AI accelerator, HBM or EUV capability.
3. The proposed 50 MW AI data-center threshold is a legislative definition, not operational capacity.
4. Grid connection and planning permission must be separate capabilities in the model.
5. Knesset summaries contain conflicting requested-connection metrics, which must remain dated and unresolved until regulator material is obtained.
6. Decision 4255 is officially corroborated at the policy-direction level, while the full official text remains pending.
7. AI21 is an Israel-origin and Israel-based model capability, but country of ultimate control is not verified by the available company sources.
8. Water, cooling, backup-generation and fuel-continuity evidence remains incomplete.
