Status: research release candidate, not ready for public citation.

- Corrected TSMC Arizona planned investment from USD 265 billion to USD 165 billion.
- Separated the operational first TSMC Arizona fab from later planned fabs and packaging facilities.
- Replaced Israel's unaudited 74% gas-electricity metric for 2025 with the audited statement that more than 70% of electricity was gas-generated in 2024.

- Expanded Israel's semiconductor representation into design, metrology and inspection, fabrication and an aggregate ecosystem.

- Kept future U.S. Micron HBM packaging in planned status.
- Added U.S. data-center electricity consumption in TWh and explicitly marked 2028 values as forecasts.
- Downgraded AI21's strict domestic-control classification pending a suitable primary corporate source.
- Marked Government Decision 4255 as partially corroborated while a stable official full-text source is pending.
